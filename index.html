How to Make a PB&J

  1. Gather Materials.
    1. Plate
    2. Knife
    3. Bread
    4. Peanut Butter
    5. Jelly
  2. Take two slices of bread and place them on the plate.
  3. Open peanut butter and use the knife to spread it evenly on one of the bread slices.
  4. Open jelly and use the knife to spread it evenly on the other bread slice.
  5. Put the bread slices together with the peanut butter and jelly sides facing eachother.
  6. Finally, use the knife to cut the sandwich down the middle.
  7. Enjoy!
