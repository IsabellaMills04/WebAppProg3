Superhero Team

Incandescent Manta Ray and her sidekick Iris

Incandescent Manta Ray glides through air and water, glowing softly to light dark places and disorient stealthy enemies.

Iris, her sidekick, commands fast-growing vines and pollen clouds. She's quick, scrappy, and great at making cover out of nothing.

Foes

  • Dr. Umbra
    • Major power: Blankets a city block in total darkness for ten minutes.
    • Minor power: Makes wall shadows whisper unsettling rumors.
  • The Rust Widow
    • Major power: Corrodes any metal she touches in seconds.
    • Minor power: Leaves a rust-colored fingerprint smudge wherever she's been.
